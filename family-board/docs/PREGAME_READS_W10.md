# 2026 Week 10: blind pre-game reads

Every read below uses only what was known before that game kicked off: the closing line, earlier results, the final pre-game injury report, the pre-kickoff depth chart, and TD chances from model usage-v0.3c refit on earlier games only. No final scores, results or later developments are used. Generated by `research/pregame_reads.py`.

**How to read a game.** *Favored* is the betting line's favorite. *Lean* compares the line with a simple margin rating (earlier point margins, 2025 at quarter weight, home field 1.5 pts); a lean is shown only when the two differ by 3+ points, and it is a cross-check, not a proven edge. Spreads of 2.5 or less are called close. TD chances are the model's pre-game estimates (main reason in brackets); players listed Out or Doubtful on the final report are excluded.
